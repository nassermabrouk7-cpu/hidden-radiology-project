Pneumonia — Clinical Case

45-year-old patient with cough and fever for 5 days.

What to Look For

Localized opacity in the right lower lobe with air bronchograms.

Common Mistake

Confusing pneumonia with pleural effusion.

Quality & Safety Note

Ensure PA positioning, not AP, if the patient is conscious.

Takeaway

Localized opacity + air bronchograms = pneumonia.
